📱 Show in Phone Share List

To make ClipSync appear in your phone's native Share list (just like WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.), follow this simple step:

🤖 Android (Google Chrome):

1. Open this website in Chrome on your phone.

2. Tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) at top-right ➔ tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").

3. Done! Android will instantly register ClipSync in your system Share list. Whenever you share any text or link from Twitter, YouTube, Chrome, etc., choose ClipSync!

🍏 iPhone (iOS Safari):

1. Open this website in Safari.

2. Tap the Share button (⎙) at the bottom ➔ tap "Add to Home Screen".
